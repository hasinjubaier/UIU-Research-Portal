Platform Features

Everything You Need to Research Together

14 integrated modules built specifically for UIU's academic research community.

01
Collaboration Finder
Post your project idea and discover teammates filtered by department, skills, and research topic.
02
Project Workspace
Kanban boards, milestones, file sharing, and built-in chat — a complete project management tool.
03
Research Editor
Collaborative writing with version history, comments, and citation support in IEEE and APA formats.
04
Resource Hub
Upload and discover papers, PDFs, and study materials with recommendations based on your interests.
05
Dataset Hub
Share datasets, code, and experiment results with version control — a mini research GitHub for UIU.
06
Messaging System
Direct messages, project group chats, and discussion rooms for every research team.
07
Blog and Knowledge Sharing
Publish research summaries and tutorials. Build your academic portfolio with every post.
08
Contribution Tracking
Automatically tracks edits, uploads, and task completions — resolving fairness issues in teamwork.
09
Reputation and Leaderboard
Earn points for completing projects, sharing resources, and helping peers. Climb the rankings.
10
Innovation Hub
Post research ideas, gather collaborators, and upvote innovations from the UIU community.
11
Events and Opportunities
Find hackathons, conferences, and competitions. Never miss an academic research opportunity.
12
Security and Trust
University email verification, public and private project control, and a community trust system.
How It Works

From Idea to Published Research

A simple five-step journey designed around how researchers actually work.

1
Create Profile
Register with your UIU email and add your skills, research interests, and academic bio.
2
Find Collaborators
Browse or post a project idea to find teammates by department and skill set.
3
Work Together
Use the workspace to assign tasks, share files, chat, and co-author the research paper.
4
Share Resources
Upload datasets, code, and papers to the resource hubs for the wider community.
5
Build Your Portfolio
Publish blogs, earn reputation points, receive badges, and grow your academic standing.
