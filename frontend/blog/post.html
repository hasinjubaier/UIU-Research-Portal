A Beginner's Guide to Writing a Research Paper

RA
Rafsan Ahmed
CSE · 3rd Year · Apr 10, 2025
43 comments

Writing your first research paper is one of the most daunting tasks as a university student. After struggling through my own first paper during my 2nd year at UIU, I put together this comprehensive guide to help fellow students navigate the entire process — from choosing a topic to final submission.

1. Choose a Research Topic

Your topic should be specific enough to be manageable but broad enough to have sufficient literature. Ask yourself:

• What am I genuinely curious about?
• Is there existing research I can build upon?
• Do I have access to the necessary data or tools?

"The best research topic is one that you find genuinely fascinating — curiosity is the engine of good research."

2. Conduct a Literature Review

Before writing a single word of your paper, you need to understand what has already been done. Use Google Scholar, IEEE Xplore, and PubMed to find related work. Read at least 20-30 papers in your area.

Use a tool like Zotero or Mendeley to manage your references from the start — you will thank yourself later.

3. Define Your Research Question

A clear, focused research question is the backbone of your paper. It should be:

• Specific — not "AI in healthcare" but "CNN-based pneumonia detection from chest X-rays"
• Measurable — you need a way to evaluate your results
• Achievable — within your time and resource constraints

4. Structure Your Paper

Most research papers follow the IMRaD structure:

Abstract
1. Introduction
2. Related Work / Literature Review
3. Methodology
4. Experiments / Results
5. Discussion
6. Conclusion
References

5. Write the Paper

Here's a counterintuitive tip: don't write the Abstract first. Write your Methodology and Results sections first — those are the concrete parts you know best. Then write the Introduction and Related Work, and finally write the Abstract last.

6. Citations & References

Always use a citation style appropriate for your field. At UIU's CSE department, we typically use IEEE style. The UIU Research Portal's built-in editor supports automatic IEEE and APA formatting.

7. Review and Revise

Never submit a first draft. Let teammates review it through the Research Portal's collaborative editor. Pay attention to clarity, logical flow, and grammar. Run it through Grammarly and Turnitin before submission.
